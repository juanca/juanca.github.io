Never miss street cleaning again.

Curbwise saves where you parked, finds the sweeping schedule for that block, and reminds you before the sweeper comes — so the only thing on your windshield is your wipers.

Coming soon · iPhone · CarPlay · Siri

What it does

One tap to save

Tap the button when you park. Curbwise grabs your location and looks up the block from San Francisco's open data.

Smart reminders

Gets a notification fifteen minutes before the next sweep so you have time to move the car — or coffee, or whatever.

Pick your block

GPS isn't always perfect, especially between buildings. The picker shows nearby candidates with their schedule so you confirm the right curb.

Built for the car

Full CarPlay support. Save before you leave the seat, or auto-save the moment you unplug.

Hey Siri, I parked

Hands-free voice trigger captures your spot and tells you when the sweeper's coming.

Works offline

The whole city's sweeping schedule is cached on your phone. No data signal, no problem.

How it works

1

Park

Tap save in the app, in CarPlay, or ask Siri.

2

Confirm the block

Curbwise shows nearby curbs with their sweeping schedule. Tap yours.

3

Get reminded

A notification arrives before the next sweep. Move the car. Done.

Heads up

Curbwise is San Francisco only for the first release, and covers street sweeping plus posted time limits. Color curbs (red, yellow, white, green, blue) and metered parking aren't covered yet — both are on the roadmap. Built on the city's DataSF open feeds.
