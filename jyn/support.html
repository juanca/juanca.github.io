In danger right now? Call 911 first. Every chat has a Safety kit (the shield at the top) with Call 911, Report, Block and Leave.

My sign-in code didn't arrive

  • Check the number, including the area code. jyn texts a 6-digit code to it.
  • Wait a minute and tap Resend code. If you ask for many codes in a row, jyn makes you wait a little before sending more.

I don't see any wishes

  • jyn needs your location to show what's near you. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → jyn is set to "While Using the App".
  • Wishes are short-lived and local: they reach about 5 miles at first, and none run longer than a day. "You're all caught up" means you've seen everything nearby right now. Pull down to check again later.

My wish disappeared

Every wish runs out when its timer does, from 30 minutes to 24 hours, whatever you picked. If nobody took it on, open it and tap Repost: it's free, gets a fresh timer, and reaches a little further each time (5, 10, 20, then 50 miles).

Why can't I add someone as a friend?

You can add someone once you've met through jyn: one of you granted the other's wish, you went to the same Join me, or you scanned each other's code in person (Me → Friends → My code / Scan).

Is there any money involved?

No. jyn is free, and nothing is paid between neighbors. The give-back on a wish is a thank-you, like "coffee"; amounts of money are taken out of it.

How do I report or block someone?

In a chat, tap the shield for the Safety kit. On a wish or a profile, use the ⋯ menu. Reports are confidential: the person isn't told who reported them. Blocking someone ends your chat with them, hides their wishes from you, and stops them from seeing your posts or messaging you. They aren't told.

I'm not getting notifications

  • Check Settings → Notifications → jyn and make sure notifications are allowed.
  • Wish alerts (new wishes nearby) have their own settings in the app: Me → Settings → Wish alerts, where you choose how far, which kinds, and quiet hours.
  • jyn doesn't notify you about a chat you have open.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Settings → Delete Account. It's immediate and can't be undone. The privacy policy says exactly what's removed.
