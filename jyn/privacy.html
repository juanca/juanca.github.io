Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

jyn helps you ask people nearby for a hand and help them back. That only works if the app knows roughly where you are and lets you talk to the people who offer, so jyn has an account and a server. This page says what it keeps, who sees what, and what happens when you leave.

  • Other people see your first name and last initial, never your phone number.
  • Other people see your wish's spot to about 100 metres. The exact place goes only to someone you share it with in a chat.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no money moves through jyn.

What you give jyn

  • Your phone number. You sign in with a code texted to it. It's how jyn knows you're you; nobody else on jyn sees it.
  • Your name. Others see it as first name and last initial ("Ada L.").
  • Your location, while the app is open. jyn asks for location "While Using the App" only, never in the background. It keeps one position per person, rounded to about 100 metres, replacing the last one; there's no history of where you've been. It's used to show you wishes near you and to tell people how far away a wish is.
  • What you post. Your wishes and Join me activities (what you need, the details, an optional give-back, and the spot you pick), your offers on other people's wishes, your chat messages, and the thank-yous you write.
  • Friends, reports and blocks. Who you've met through jyn or scanned in person, your friends and requests, and any report or block you make.
  • Notification settings and a device token, so jyn can send you push notifications, and your wish-alert preferences (how far, which kinds, quiet hours).

The camera is used only to scan a friend's code when you meet in person; nothing it sees is kept or sent.

What other people see

  • Your first name and last initial, your trust badge ("New here" or "Granted N"), your trust score, when you joined, and the thank-yous people left you.
  • Your wishes, with their spot rounded to about 100 metres and distances to a tenth of a mile.
  • Offers on your wish are private to you; others see only how many there are.
  • If you're friends, whether you're online and whether you're nearby (within 5 miles).
  • Messages in a chat are seen by the people in that chat. A location you share in a chat is exact, and only that chat sees it.

Nobody is told that you reported or blocked them.

Who else handles your data

  • Twilio receives your phone number to text you sign-in codes.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your phone. A notification can include a preview of a chat message.
  • Fly.io hosts jyn's server and database in the United States.

jyn has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries, and sells or rents your data to no one.

How long it's kept

  • Your account stays until you delete it.
  • Wishes and chats are kept after they end, so you can look back at them; old chats move to Archived but aren't deleted. You can delete a message you sent.
  • Sign-in sessions last up to 30 days. Delivered notifications are cleared from the server after 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete Account. It happens straight away and can't be undone:

  • Your phone number, name, friends, requests, the thank-yous you wrote and received, your location, your notification settings and devices are removed, and you're signed out everywhere.
  • The messages you sent are replaced with "Message deleted", and your chats show that you deleted your account.
  • Your live wishes are cancelled. The wishes themselves (their text and spot) and the name shown on them at the time are kept, without your phone number or account details.
  • Reports other people made about you are kept, for safety.

Children

jyn is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything significant will be called out in the app's release notes.
